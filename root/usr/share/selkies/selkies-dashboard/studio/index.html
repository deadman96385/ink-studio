FROM DESIGN TO STITCHES

Your embroidery workbench.

Inkscape and Ink/Stitch run on your computer. Your iPad controls the live workspace, and your files stay saved on the PC.

  1. Create or open an SVG. File → New starts a 100 × 100 mm PP1 page. Start lettering with the Lettering button. To edit existing Ink/Stitch text, select its lettering group first.
  2. Set up the stitches. Select your shapes, then tap Stitch settings. Preview stitches previews the selection; deselect everything to preview the whole design.
  3. Save the master, export a copy. Tap Save SVG. Tap Export PES…, choose “Ink/Stitch: Brother Embroidery Format (.pes)” in the file type list, and save under /designs.
  4. Download and sew. Open Files, download the PES to iPad Files, then transfer it using your embroidery machine’s supported method.

Comfortable on the iPad

Use landscape. Tap directly or turn on Trackpad for precise cursor movement. In trackpad mode: tap to click, tap and hold to drag, two fingers to scroll or right-click. Keyboard opens text entry. The small tab on the left opens screen and file-transfer settings.

Keep the actual stitch bounds within 100 × 100 mm. Resize the SVG and regenerate the PES when changing the finished size. Leaving this page keeps the session running; save before restarting the PC.
